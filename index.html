How to play
  • Everyone hunts, everyone is hunted. You get a contract on another player. Someone else has one on you.
  • The streets are full of people dressed exactly like you. Walk like a local and nobody can tell you apart.
  • The compass around you points at your mark. When you're close enough they light up gold — get next to them and press E.
  • Hit the wrong person and you lose the contract. Sprinting near your mark exposes you.
  • Feel a hunter on your back? Spot them and press E first to klap them.
  • Bonus points for staying low profile, blending into a crowd or under a tree, and never getting spotted.
