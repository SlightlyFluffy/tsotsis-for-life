How to play
  • Everyone hunts, everyone is hunted. You get a contract on another player. Someone else has one on you.
  • The streets are full of people dressed exactly like you. Walk like a local and nobody can tell you apart.
  • The compass only gives a rough direction. Up close it goes quiet, so pick your mark out by their outfit, then watch them for a moment to identify them.
ClickKill · 5 pts Click your contract when you're right next to them. Walking only: running strikes fail.
R-ClickPoison · 2 pts Silent. They don't know, and they drop 5s later, giving you time to walk away. 25s cooldown.
ClickKlap · 3 pts Click someone hunting you to stun them and wreck their contract.
EClone Everyone near you takes on your look for 15s. Breaks your hunters' lock. 30s cooldown.
FDecoy Someone near you, dressed as you, bolts. Your hunters lose track of you. 20s cooldown.
QKick dust Stuns everyone right next to you for 2.5s. Your escape. 20s cooldown.
ShiftRun Fast but loud. Run near your target and they'll see you coming.

Hiding spots (mielie patches, skip bins, laundry lines): walk in and nobody can see you. You can strike out of them, and you can click a spot to strike whoever is hiding inside. Civilians wander through them too, so be careful.

Hit the wrong person: −1 point, no strikes for 6s, no contract for 15s, and the scream gives you away to your hunters.

  • Finding your target: the card at the top left shows who they are and what they're wearing. Far away the wedge gives a rough direction. Up close the compass goes quiet (just a pulse), so look for their outfit. Watch them for 1.5s to identify them (gold ring). You can't identify someone blending in, under a tree or in a hiding spot.
  • Hiding spots (mielie patches, skip bins, laundry lines): walk in and nobody can see you. You can strike out of them, and you can click a spot to strike whoever is hiding inside. Civilians wander through them too, so be careful.
  • Kill bonuses: +1 low profile, +2 blended in or from hiding, +1 never spotted.
  • Wrong person (a civilian or the wrong player): −1, no strikes for 6s, no new contract for 15s, and the scream reveals you to your hunters for 5s. A wrong poison is silent, but you still lose the contract.
  • Striking: you hit exactly who you click (or tap), and only if they're right next to you. A white ring shows who a click would hit.
  • Civilians are unpredictable: they turn back, stop dead, jog, and sometimes tail people. Don't assume.
  • Being hunted: the bottom-right card shows how many are after you and how close. Anyone who runs at you gets marked red.
